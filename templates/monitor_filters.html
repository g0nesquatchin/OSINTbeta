Display filters

Applied at render time on the matches table — they hide rows but never drop data from the DB. To see what's currently being hidden, open /monitor?bypass_filters=1.

Domain blocklist

One host per line. Matches the canonical host exactly OR as a subdomain — msn.com blocks msn.com and news.msn.com and www.msn.com. Leading www. on your input is stripped automatically.

Minimum title length

Drop rows whose title is shorter than this many characters. Useful when broken RSS parsing produces empty or single-word titles. Set to 0 to disable.
