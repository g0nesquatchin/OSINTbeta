How this works

Powered by GDELT's Global Knowledge Graph (GKG). Every 15 minutes GDELT publishes a new file of every article it has processed, tagged with the locations mentioned in the article — not just the country of the outlet. We download and parse those files locally so you get true town-level pins, not country centroids.

First search after startup may take a few seconds while the first file downloads. Searches against already-fetched data are instant.
